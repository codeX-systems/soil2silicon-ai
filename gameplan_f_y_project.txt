1. Designing the workflow of the app.
2. Dividing the different workflow processes into tasks.
3. These tasks will be spread across phases.
4. Deciding the number of phases.
5. Each phase will have iteration of certian procedures.
6. Agile methodology will be followed for each phase.
7. Each sprint of agile methodology will have Requirements->Development->Testing->Feedback->Development/Stop.
8. Duration of each sprint and phase will be decided in advance.
9. After each sprint copy of code and other assets will be uploaded to github.
10. Version will be updated on git at a gap of atleast 1 sprint. (Eg: Sprint 2 version will be updated to Sprint 3 version on github after completion of Sprint 4)
11. After major updates, stable version will be rolled out for real world users to test.
12. Their feedback will be recorded through forms and analysed thoroughly.
13. Suggestions and or improvements may be taken into account after the current and the next sprint.
14. All libraries, frameworks and languages used will have a proper version noted in hardcopy and softcopy.
15. No change in any versions of tech-stack will happen without the completion of a major Phase.
